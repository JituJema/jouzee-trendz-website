Sera ya Kurudisha Bidhaa na MalipoReturn & Payment Policy

1. Malipo

Malipo yote hufanyika kupitia Airtel Money kabla ya bidhaa kutumwa, isipokuwa kama imekubaliwa vinginevyo na Jouzee Trendz.

2. Kurudisha Bidhaa

  • Bidhaa inaweza kurudishwa ndani ya siku 2 baada ya kupokelewa, endapo ina hitilafu ya kiwandani au haikulingana na maelezo yaliyotolewa.
  • Bidhaa lazima irudishwe ikiwa haijatumika, na vitambulisho/lebo vikiwa bado vimeambatanishwa.
  • Gharama za usafirishaji wa kurudisha bidhaa hulipwa na mteja, isipokuwa kama hitilafu ilitokana na Jouzee Trendz.

3. Kubadilisha Bidhaa (Exchange)

Ubadilishaji wa saizi au rangi unawezekana ndani ya siku 2, kutegemeana na upatikanaji wa bidhaa.

4. Marejesho ya Fedha (Refund)

Marejesho ya fedha hutolewa kupitia Airtel Money ndani ya siku 3-5 za kazi baada ya bidhaa iliyorudishwa kukaguliwa na kukubaliwa.

5. Mawasiliano

Kwa maombi yoyote ya kurudisha bidhaa, wasiliana nasi kupitia WhatsApp: 0654 701 189.

1. Payment

All payments are made via Airtel Money before goods are dispatched, unless otherwise agreed with Jouzee Trendz.

2. Returns

  • Items may be returned within 2 days of receipt if there is a manufacturing defect or the item does not match the description provided.
  • Items must be returned unused, with original tags/labels attached.
  • Return shipping costs are borne by the customer, unless the fault was caused by Jouzee Trendz.

3. Exchanges

Size or color exchanges are possible within 2 days, subject to product availability.

4. Refunds

Refunds are issued via Airtel Money within 3-5 business days after the returned item is inspected and approved.

5. Contact

For any return requests, contact us via WhatsApp: 0654 701 189.
